What on RTP Play has Portuguese subtitles?

These are the programmes whose latest episode on RTP Play has a Portuguese subtitle track — the one RTP calls Português adaptado. To turn it on, press CC at the bottom right of the player.

Series and recurring programmes carry a description of the show; one-off films and documentaries carry the description RTP gives that title. Each title and description has an English translation underneath — hide them with the checkbox below to test your reading first.

Caveats worth knowing

  • Only the latest episode of each programme was checked, so a show may have subtitles on some episodes and not others.
  • “Portugal-only licence” means RTP stores the video as licensed for Portugal only, so it won't play from abroad. Titles without the label usually play outside Portugal, but not always: in a check from Spain, about 1 in 12 of them were still blocked. Rights also change over time.
  • For foreign-language films and series the Portuguese subtitles are a translation, not a transcription of the audio — useful for reading, not for listening.
  • RTP's catalogue changes constantly. The list is rebuilt every week; treat it as a snapshot.
