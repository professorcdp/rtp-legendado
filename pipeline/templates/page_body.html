What on RTP Play has Portuguese subtitles?

These are the programmes whose latest episode on RTP Play has a Portuguese subtitle track (the one RTP calls Português adaptado). To turn it on, click the CC button at the bottom right of the player.

Series and recurring programmes carry a description of the show; one-off films and documentaries carry the description RTP gives that title. Each title and description has an English translation underneath it, in italics. You can hide the translations with the checkbox below the search box.

How the list was made: I checked all 6,268 TV programmes in the RTP Play catalogue, looking at the latest episode of each for a subtitle file. Portuguese-language shows come first. International documentaries and foreign-language fiction are at the bottom, because there the subtitles are usually a translation.

Caveats

Only the latest episode of each programme was checked. Some shows add subtitles to only some episodes (older lists mention Portugueses pelo Mundo and Luz Vermelha, for example). So a show can be missing here and still have subtitles on some episodes, and a show listed here may not have them on every episode.

Many older titles are still listed in RTP's catalogue but may have expired from the player. The "episodes" figure is how many episodes the programme page links to, and the page stops counting at about 12. Subtitles are closed captions for deaf and hard-of-hearing viewers, so expect sound cues in capitals, like RISOS or MÚSICA.

Playability was checked on 11 Sep 2026 against RTP's own rights service, from your computer's connection (RTP located it in Spain). Rights depend on the viewer's country and change over time. A programme can play in Portugal but be blocked abroad, and new episodes sometimes get different rights from old ones. Untick the box to see everything, with blocked titles marked.

Programmes whose subtitle file was empty, plus one-off institutional items like Christmas messages and trailers, were left out.
