What on RTP Play has Portuguese captions?

These are the programs whose latest episode on RTP Play has Portuguese captions (same-language subtitles, what RTP calls Português adaptado). To turn them on, press CC at the bottom right of the player.

Series and recurring programs carry a description of the show; one-off films and documentaries carry the description RTP gives that title. Each title and description has an English translation underneath — hide them with the checkbox below to test your reading first.

All {{CHECKED_N}} TV programs in the catalog were checked, looking at the latest episode of each for a caption file. Portuguese-language shows come first; international documentaries and foreign-language fiction are at the bottom, because there the subtitles are a translation rather than a transcript.

Caveats worth knowing

  • Only the latest episode of each program was checked, so a show may have captions on some episodes and not others.
  • “Portugal-only license” means RTP stores the video as licensed for Portugal only, so it won't play from abroad. Titles without the label usually play outside Portugal, but not always: in a check from Spain, about 1 in 12 of them were still blocked. Rights also change over time.
  • For foreign-language films and series the Portuguese subtitles are a translation, not captions that transcribe the audio — useful for reading, not for listening.
  • RTP's catalog changes constantly. The list is rebuilt every week; treat it as a snapshot.
